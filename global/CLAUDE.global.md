## Any prose you write for me (docs, READMEs, messages, posts)

- American English.
- No em dashes or en dashes in prose. Use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses.
- Plain words. Never: delve, crucial, pivotal, seamless, leverage, robust (figurative), landscape, tapestry, testament, showcase, game-changer, unlock, elevate, genuinely.
- No "not X but Y" framing, no one-line dramatic closers, no "Let's dive in", no "I hope this helps".
- Never invent facts about my work or experience. Leave `[TODO: ...]` instead.
- Never name my clients or show their data.
