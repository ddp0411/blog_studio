# Pre-publish checklist

Copy into the post folder as `checklist.md` and tick as you go. Items marked (auto) are checked by `scripts/ai_tells.py`.

## Before writing
- [ ] Format chosen: short opinion (3 to 5 min) or complete guide (10+ min). Not the muddy middle.
- [ ] Topic is timely and comes from something I actually did

## Title and subtitle
- [ ] Title is a question or bold claim, 8 to 12 plain words (auto: word count)
- [ ] Title has a specific number or result, if I have one
- [ ] The post delivers exactly what the title promises
- [ ] Subtitle teases the answer or adds the missing detail, under 15 words

## Opening
- [ ] First two lines: a scene, number, comparison, surprising result, or question
- [ ] Says why I'm the one writing this
- [ ] The hook works inside the first 150 words

## Body
- [ ] 3 to 6 sentence-case headings, written as mini-stories or numbered steps
- [ ] Paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences (auto: flags 5+)
- [ ] First person, honest about at least one mistake, one or two dry jokes at most
- [ ] Every number has a source (my notes, a linked doc, a survey)
- [ ] Cover image with caption; screenshots or code for technical parts
- [ ] No client names, schemas, or internal numbers

## Sounds like me
- [ ] Humanizer pass done with voice samples
- [ ] Zero em/en dashes (auto)
- [ ] Zero banned words and phrases (auto)
- [ ] American spelling (auto)
- [ ] At most one "not X but Y" contrast (auto)
- [ ] Zero [TODO] markers (auto)
- [ ] Read aloud once; nothing sounds like a press release

## Ending
- [ ] Ends on a takeaway or quotable line that adds something new
- [ ] Asks readers one specific question
- [ ] Links one or two related earlier posts
- [ ] CTA: newsletter, LinkedIn, GitHub (if code)

## Publishing
- [ ] Published on devanshupatil.blog first; URL copied
- [ ] Medium: imported or canonical set; 5 topics; publication submitted; free
- [ ] Dev.to: canonical_url, 4 tags, cover 1000x420
- [ ] Hashnode: canonical set, subtitle, tags, SEO description
- [ ] LinkedIn: native post, link in first comment
- [ ] Calendar reminder to run /stats-review in 7 days
