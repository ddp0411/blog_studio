# Voice samples

Humanizer matches rhythm, word choice, and punctuation from real samples of my writing. This folder is where they live.

## What to put here

2 to 4 files, each with 2 or 3 paragraphs I wrote myself (not AI-edited). Good sources:

- The opening of "Setting up Claude Code properly" or "Five things I got wrong building an offline RAG system"
- A long Slack/WhatsApp message where I explained something technical to a friend
- A LinkedIn post I wrote by hand

Name them `sample-01.md`, `sample-02.md`, and so on.

## Before saving a sample

1. Replace every em dash with a comma, period, colon, or parentheses. (Humanizer copies the dash rate of the samples. We want zero.)
2. Delete "genuinely".
3. Leave everything else as I wrote it, including small mistakes and odd phrasing. Those are the point.

## Every few months

Swap in a newer sample from a post I'm happy with, so the voice keeps up with me.
